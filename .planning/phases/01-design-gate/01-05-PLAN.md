Facts to show at the checkpoint (from research, measured):
- Sofia Sans Extra Condensed is the only face whose Display words fit everywhere ("Achievements" on a 390px phone, "Involvement" in cols 7-12), but its YAHYA is height-bound at 1366x768 (not edge to edge on laptops), and the route needs two files (Extra Condensed + Sofia Sans for body).
- Instrument Sans maxes at weight 700 and has a wdth 75-100 axis: one file can give a condensed YAHYA and normal body. Research flags it as common on 2024-25 startup sites (taste call).
- Hanken Grotesk has no `tnum` feature but its figures are tabular by default (passes D-06).
- Inter Tight carries the anti-vibe flag; it can win only on the trial, with a one-sentence reason.
- Söhne variable comes only with the full family (likely over $300); under budget means 2 static weights. Klim web licences allow only Klim's own WOFF2 on a domain you own; test fonts must never be used live or committed.
</context>
