## Spacing Scale

Unchanged from 03-UI-SPEC; all multiples of 4.

| Token | Value | New usage in this phase |
|-------|-------|-------------------------|
| xs | 4px | Preview caption line gap; deep-dive meta to lead nudges; walkthrough button hit padding (vertical, fine pointer) |
| sm | 8px | Preview image to caption; walkthrough controls under the frame; inline thumbnail under its line; landscape hint padding |
| md | 16px | Deep-dive paragraph gap; landscape hint below the phone section list; About portrait toggle hint offset |
| lg | 24px | Walkthrough control gap; deep-dive section rule to content; gap between stacked deep-dive screenshots (equals gutter); margin names inset from header and footer |
| xl | 32px | Deep-dive title to lead; phone gap between Achievements lines (with thumbnails); phone spiral band top margin |
| 2xl | 48px | About rings end 48px above the text; phone gap between stacked blocks |
| 3xl | 64px | Deep-dive gap between sections and after the lead; deep-dive bottom padding above the footer |

Exceptions (all multiples of 4 or line weights):
- Touch: every new control (walkthrough Pause/Previous/Next, Pause motion, portrait toggle, Back to Projects, deep-dive links) has a 44x44px minimum hit area under `(pointer: coarse)` via padding or `::before`, never overlapping a neighbour. Fine pointers keep `min-height: 24px` (v7).
- Achievements preview nudge `--pv-y` is a measured offset (keeps the preview 8px inside the viewport bottom, above the footer), not a spacing step.
- The edge-cropped "03" and the spiral overrun the page edge on purpose (design doc §4.2); both are clipped with `overflow: clip`, never scroll.
- Hairline 1px, progress bar 2px, focus ring 2px / offset 4px are line weights.
