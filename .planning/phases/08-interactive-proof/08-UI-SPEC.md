## Spacing Scale

Unchanged from 03/06-UI-SPEC; all multiples of 4.

| Token | Value | New usage in this phase |
|-------|-------|-------------------------|
| xs | 4px | Link to its dim source line; stacked meta lines on phones |
| sm | 8px | Control bar to the stage edge (above the Atlantis stage, below the robot stage); progress bar inset none (it sits on the stage's bottom edge) |
| md | 16px | Gap between the two links in a Projects row head; paragraph gap in the side column |
| lg | 24px | Gap between controls in a control row; title `padding-top`; stage to the screenshot strip; gap between links in the side column (Facturo pattern) |
| xl | 32px | Title to the control bar / stage; side column lines to its links |
| 2xl | 48px | Phone: stage block to the text block |
| 3xl | 64px | Bottom padding above the footer (inherited `.main`) |

Exceptions (all multiples of 4 or line weights):
- Touch: every new control ("Play Atlantis", "Exit game", "Mute", "Reset view", "Pause motion", the new row links "Play" and "3D model", side-column links) has a 44x44px minimum hit area under `(pointer: coarse)`, never overlapping a neighbour. Fine pointers keep `min-height: 24px`.
- Stage maximum height on desktop is a measured fit, not a spacing step: `max-height: calc(100svh - var(--frame-top) - var(--frame-bottom) - var(--space-lg) - var(--title-h) - var(--space-xl) - var(--space-lg))`, where `--title-h` is the title's rendered line box (`var(--text-display) * 0.88`). The stage then shrinks its width to keep `--stage-aspect`, flush left. Result: title and stage always fit one screen at 1366x768 and above (NAV-07 spirit on a scrolling page).
- Hairline 1px, game progress bar 2px (same weight as the site progress bar), focus ring 2px / offset 4px (offset -2px on stages) are line weights.

Desktop: a `clamp(220px, 21vw, 360px)` band right of the title, the pictogram right-aligned and centred vertically with space around it, never over text; the title row takes the band height as `min-height`. The grey twins stay for the Projects and Achievements previews. The robot CAD still and model stay grey.

Greyscale (DSGN-05): the Atlantis poster and screenshots are pre-converted grey WebPs; the live game shows its own colours once the visitor presses Play (it is the work itself, running, like a colour twin shown on the visitor's action; not an accent use). The robot is grey in every state; it has no colour twin.

Robot shading **[discretion]**: one directional key light from the upper left (azimuth about 45 degrees left of camera, elevation about 35 degrees) plus a flat ambient term, Lambert only, no specular, no shadows, no ground plane, no outline pass. Output clamps so the unlit faces sit at `#4a4a4a` and faces square to the light reach `#e0e0e0`: the silhouette always reads on the ground and nothing glows past ink. The static still (D-14) is rendered by the same shader, camera and light at the start pose, on `#0b0b0b`, so the still-to-live handoff shows no jump.

Contrast: accent on ground 7.4:1, dim on ground 6.0:1, ink on ground 17.3:1 (all AA). The control bars sit on plain ground, never over the poster or the canvas.

- Header unchanged with `label` "02.3 Atlantis" / "02.5 Robot firmware"; rail in rail mode, "A" current.

On `/projects/robot/` only, "Pause motion" / "Play motion" in cols 4-6 flush left on the footer baseline (same button and styles as the pager footer) while auto-rotate is possible; `visibility: hidden` once the visitor has touched the model (nothing moves on its own after that), `display: none` under reduced motion and with JS off.

### Atlantis stage (`GameStage`, PROOF-01)

Order in the DOM and on screen: control bar, then stage. The control bar sits `--space-sm` above the stage, one Label row with `gap: var(--space-lg)`, left to right; "Play Atlantis" is Heading-size and sets the bar's baseline.

| State | Control bar | Stage |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| Ready (JS on, WebGL2 present, build cleared and under the weight threshold) | "Play Atlantis" (Heading button), dim Label "Loads {size} MB of game files" | Grey poster `<img>` filling the box (`object-fit: cover`). A pointer click on the poster also plays (convenience; the button is the keyboard control). Custom cursor snaps to the stage with the tag "Play" |
| Loading (after the click; wasm/pck fetched only now, D-05) | Dim status "Loading the game, {nn}%" (`tabular-nums`, polite live region, updated at most every 10%), "Exit game" already present to cancel | Poster stays; a 2px ink progress bar grows along the stage's bottom edge by `transform: scaleX()` from the left (determinate only; with no progress events, no bar and the text alone). No spinner, no pulse |
| Playing | "Exit game", "Mute"/"Unmute", dim "Esc leaves the game" | Same-origin iframe (Godot's own shell from a first-party path) fills the box. Poster fades out over `--dur-fade`; focus moves into the iframe; accent ring inside the stage edge while the iframe holds focus. Audio on (the click is the gesture, D-07) |
| Exited | Back to Ready, focus on "Play Atlantis" | Iframe removed (audio stops, memory freed), poster back instantly. Replay comes from the HTTP cache |
| Load failed | Error line (dim Body) + "Try again" (Label button) | Poster stays |
| Cannot run (no WebGL2) / too heavy / not cleared / touch fallback / JS off | Matching line from the copy table, or nothing when the build simply is not shipped | Poster only. Screenshot strip and links below are unchanged |

Interaction rules:
- **Keyboard (D-10):** Tab order is control bar, then the iframe, then the side column. Esc pressed while the game has focus moves focus to the control bar's first control ("Exit game" while playing; the parent listens on the same-origin iframe document). The game keeps running until "Exit game". No keyboard trap in any state. If the game itself binds Esc **[research]**, the parent takes Esc only when Godot does not consume it, and the hint wording follows.
- **Godot shell:** a custom export template page with `background: #0b0b0b`, the canvas filling the frame, no Godot logo, no shell progress UI (the parent owns status and the progress bar via `postMessage`), no text, no external requests. Single-thread export, no COOP/COEP.
- **Aspect:** `--stage-aspect` becomes the export's native ratio **[research]**; the canvas renders at the stage size (stretch mode chosen by research to keep pixels crisp, integer scaling preferred for a 2D game).
- Reduced motion: poster-to-game swap is instant; the progress bar jumps per update without transition. The game's own motion is the visitor's chosen content.

### Robot stage (`ModelStage`, PROOF-02)

Order: stage, then the control row `--space-sm` below it (Label, `gap: var(--space-lg)`): hint "Drag to rotate" (accent), "Reset view" (shown once the viewer is live), and on phones the dim still caption. Desktop shows the caption as the first side-column meta line instead.

| State | Stage | Control row |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| Static (server render, JS off, WebGL2 missing, chunk failed, not cleared) | Grey still `<img>` with width/height, `object-fit: contain` on ground, start pose | Nothing (or the load-failed line with click-to-load) |
| Loading | Still stays; no spinner | With click-to-load: "Loading the model" (dim) in the button's slot |
| Live | Canvas crossfades in over `--dur-fade` above the still (same pose, no visible jump), then the still is removed from the paint (kept in DOM for no-WebGL fallback paths). Slow auto-rotate, yaw only, one turn per `--model-turn` | "Drag to rotate" (accent), "Reset view" |
| Interacted | Auto-rotate has stopped for this page view and never restarts | Same; footer "Pause motion" hidden |
| Viewer focused (keyboard) | Accent ring inside the stage edge | Hint reads "Arrow keys rotate" |

Interaction rules (D-13):
- **Pointer drag (fine):** horizontal drag across the full stage width = one full yaw turn; vertical drag pitches, clamped to plus/minus 30 degrees. Rotation follows the pointer 1:1 and stops when the pointer stops: no inertia, no easing after release, no spring (motion rule).
- **Touch:** horizontal-first. `touch-action: pan-y pinch-zoom` on the canvas; the first 8px of movement decides: mostly horizontal orbits (yaw only, no pitch on touch), mostly vertical leaves the page scrolling. Never scroll-hijack, no pinch zoom of the model.
- **Keyboard:** the viewer group is one Tab stop (`tabindex="0"`). Left/Right yaw 15 degrees per press, Up/Down pitch 10 degrees per press within the clamp, Home resets. Each step animates over `--dur-ui` with `--ease-ui` (instant under reduced motion). Tab always leaves; no trap.
- **Reset view:** returns to the start pose over `--dur-page` with `--ease-page` (instant under reduced motion); does not restart auto-rotate.
- **No zoom, no pan, no wheel handling** (the wheel scrolls the page).
- **Auto-rotate runs only when** reduced motion is off, footer motion is on, the stage is in view (IntersectionObserver), the tab is visible, and the visitor has not yet touched the model (pointerdown, a key in the viewer, or Reset). "Pause motion" freezes it at its current angle; "Play motion" resumes it.
- **Load trigger [research: click vs near viewport]:** default contract is near-viewport loading (`IntersectionObserver`, `rootMargin` one viewport) with no button. If research picks click-to-load, "Rotate the model" (Heading button) sits in the control row and the hint appears only once live.

## Row-to-Deep-Dive Transition (D-17 to D-19)

One shared transition for three pairs: Facturo block / "Read the deep dive" to `/projects/facturo/`; Atlantis row / "Play" to `/projects/atlantis/`; Robot firmware row / "3D model" to `/projects/robot/`. Mechanism (cross-document View Transitions vs a client FLIP) is research's call **[research]**; this is the visual contract either must meet.

### Forward (Projects to deep dive), total `--dur-page` (600ms), `--ease-page`
1. **Surface:** a flat ground surface grows from the source box (the clicked row's ruled box; for Facturo the whole cols 1-8 block from the walkthrough top to the link) to the full content box (header bottom to footer top, rail column edge to the right margin) by animating `clip-path: inset(...)`. It covers the Projects page beneath it. No border, no shadow, the hairline is not drawn on the surface.
2. **Title:** the source name (row Heading "Atlantis" / "Robot firmware", or the Facturo Display name) moves and scales uniformly to the route's h1 position and size, anchored at its left edge and cap top (transform only, no stretch, no `font-size` animation). Only one copy of the word is ever readable: the source and destination renderings crossfade inside the first `--dur-ui` (150ms) and the destination's crisp Display glyphs carry the rest, so the width-axis change (Heading wdth 100 to Display wdth 75) never shows a double image.
3. **Everything else on Projects** (other rows, walkthrough, the clicked row's lines and links): fades out over `--dur-ui` at the start.
4. **Deep-dive body** (control bar, stage, side column, screenshot strip, footer): fades in over `--dur-fade`, starting at `--dur-page` minus `--dur-fade` (200ms), so it lands with the title. Opacity only, no slide.
5. **Persistent chrome:** the header and the rail do not move or fade (they keep their own transition identity); the header label text crossfades "02 Projects" to "02.3 Atlantis" over `--dur-ui`.

### Reverse ("Back to Projects" link, and browser Back where the mechanism supports traversal)
- Mirror of the forward run on the same tokens: deep-dive body fades out over `--dur-ui`; the surface shrinks by `clip-path` back to the row's box, revealing the Projects page at full opacity underneath; the title scales back into the row name, the crisp row Heading taking over in the last `--dur-ui`.
- Projects restores its state first: the pager lands on Projects with no opening animation and no first-visit hint (a view that starts from a site transition never plays the opening), and on phones the page's inner scroll is restored so the source row is in view before the transition starts. If the source row is not in view, there is no morph: a plain `--dur-fade` crossfade.

### Rules
- **Motion exception (D-19a):** scale growth breaks the one-motion rule on purpose (user-chosen). It stays fast (600ms), no bounce, no overshoot (`--ease-page` has no control point above 1), animates only transform, opacity and clip-path. Logged in PROJECT.md Key Decisions alongside the D-12 accent cue.
- **Reduced motion, unsupported browser (Safari or LinkedIn in-app without the mechanism), JS off:** navigation is an instant change; no-JS is a plain link. No partial or degraded animation.
- **Interruption (D-19d):** any click, key, wheel or Back during the transition finishes it instantly (the new state shows at once); nothing queues. No element is left inert, hidden, clipped or transformed afterwards.
- **Focus:** forward lands focus on the deep-dive h1 (Phase 4 rule). Reverse lands focus on the link that opened the deep dive when it is known for this history entry, else on the Projects page title. Screen readers hear the new page title; nothing is announced twice.
- **Walkthrough:** on forward it freezes on its current frame for the snapshot; on reverse it resumes its normal rules.
- **Budget (D-19e):** CSS-first. Any script is shared by all three pairs and counted in the 150 kB initial gate or kept as one lazy chunk under the 12 kB cap.
- **Facturo (D-18):** its link, Back link, tests and visual baselines are updated; the route works fully without the transition.
